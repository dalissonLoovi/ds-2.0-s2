## Rules

- **job:** Onboarding vehicle validation/confirmation display — not Home status summary
- **ctaOutside:** Confirm CTA lives on the screen, not inside this card
- **plateHero:** Plate is the primary typographic hierarchy (heading/h1), brandModel is secondary
- **sheetTopo:** topo bar uses color/background-surface/brand with centered handle (color/elevation/inverse-4, 43×4, radius/full) — sheet chrome, not AppHeader
- **stroke:** Root stroke binds color/elevation/disabled at ~32% opacity (weight 2, inside) — soft blue edge, not color/border/2
- **noIllustration:** Illustration SLOT removed — plate + brandModel only; use VehicleSummaryCard when vehicle art is required
- **vsVehicleSummaryCard:** Use VehicleSummaryCard for Home/Painel (status ChipTag + coverages + elevated surface + illustration)
- **vsOfferProductCard:** Use OfferProductCard for add-on offers with price + CTA
- **vsCardHorizontal:** Do not force CardHorizontal — Avatar + header/subhead + 80 media does not match plate-hero anatomy
- **usage:** Do not use as VehicleSummaryCard, OfferProductCard, CardHorizontal, CardFilledItem alone, or Banner
- **spacingBound:** All zero paddings/gaps bind spacing/0 (root, topo horizontal, text vertical)
